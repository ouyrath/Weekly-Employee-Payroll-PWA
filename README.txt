Weekly Employee Payroll PWA

Files:
- index.html
- app.js
- manifest.webmanifest
- sw.js
- icons/

Features:
- Installable PWA on iPhone/Android/desktop when served over HTTPS
- Works offline after first visit
- Saves employees, rates, hours and saved weeks on the device
- Overtime after 40 hours at 1.5x (toggle)
- Optional deductions
- Print weekly payroll

To publish: upload this folder to any HTTPS static host such as Vercel.
